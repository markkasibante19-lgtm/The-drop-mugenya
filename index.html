More than a venue

The Drop Muyenga is built on community, inclusivity, and real change. Located on Tank Hill Road, we bring together co‑working, bold flavours, and a social mission that matters.

Every meal & desk booking helps provide safe water to Ugandan communities. Majority of our profits are reinvested into water sanitation & hygiene (WASH) projects with SPOUTS Impact.
